### **Chapter Ten: On the Rare Sayings of the Wise**  

Know that the great prophets have mentioned rare sayings of the wise, and every one of their insights serves as a remedy for the soul. Thus, we have selected a few sayings worthy of this book and this chapter, each attributed to its respective sage.  

***Socrates said:*** *"My soul was ever in pursuit of nobility, and my soul did not permit me to rest. Every draught I gave to my soul in its thirst only increased its longing until I came to know the Necessary Existent. And every knowledge that was meant for me was unveiled until I was satisfied."*  

***Another sage said:*** *"Never did I dispute with anyone, for I did not speak of what I did not know. As for praise, blame, debate, or contention—none of these can serve as proof."*  

***Yet another said:*** *"How excellent is the principle of divine law in restraining pleasures! Yet in this matter, wisdom is stronger. For in divine law, pleasures are forbidden by compulsion, whereas in wisdom, they are relinquished by nature. In divine law, pleasures are abandoned through imitation, but in wisdom, through knowledge."*  

*And God knows best what is correct.*
