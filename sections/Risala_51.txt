### | فصل دهم در نوادر حكما 
بداند كه انبيا [ى] بزرگ ذكر نوادر حكما كرده‌اند و هر نكته‌اى از نكته‌هاى ايشان شفايى باشد نفس را. پس ما چند سخن كه لايق اين كتاب و اين فصل بود بياورديم از قول هر حكيمى جدايگانه.*** سقراط‍‌ گفت نفس من مادام در طلب شرف بود، و نفس من نگذاشت كه من

### Chapter Ten: On the Rare Sayings of the Sages  
Know that the great prophets have recounted the rare sayings of the sages, and every one of their insights is a remedy for the soul. Thus, we have selected a few sayings worthy of this book and this chapter, presenting them separately from each sage.  

*** Socrates said: "My soul was ever in pursuit of nobility, and it did not allow me to rest. Every draught I gave my soul in its thirst only increased its longing—until I came to know the Necessary Existent. And every knowledge that was meant for me was unveiled from that source, until I was satisfied."  

*** Another said: "I never disagreed with anyone, for I did not speak of what I did not know. And as for all praise, blame, debate, and dispute, none of it can serve as proof."  

*** Another said: "How excellent is the principle of divine law in restraining pleasures! Yet in this matter, wisdom is stronger. For in the law, pleasures are forbidden by compulsion, but in wisdom, by nature. In the law, pleasures are renounced through imitation, but in wisdom, through knowledge." And God knows best what is correct.  

### | Scribal Notes Included in the Printed Edition 
Here ends the book *Mujmal al-Hikmah* (The Compendium of Wisdom), by the grace of God—Exalted is His Name—on the last day of the noble month of Sha‘ban, in the year 667. "Sufficient for us is God, and what an excellent guardian! What an excellent master and what an excellent helper!"
