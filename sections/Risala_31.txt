### Summary of the First Epistle on Psychical Matters Concerning Rational Principles  
*In the Name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful*  

Know that the principles of a thing are that which serve as its foundation and cause in knowledge, and these have been established in many sciences. Yet one must acknowledge that it is not incumbent upon any speaker to encompass all sciences for a single individual.  

These principles are called such because they form the premises of syllogistic reasoning, and none should deny that Pythagoras was the first sage of this era to compose a book on the science of arithmetic, compiling its calculations and properties. He asserted that all existents conform to numbers, and whoever comprehends numbers—their genera, species, and properties—understands the genera and species of existent things, their quantities, and their causes. For every science is illuminated by its arithmetic exemplar.  

Thus, he declared: *God the Exalted created all things in pairs, triads, quartets, quintets, and so forth.*  

- **Dyads (Pairs)**: Such as matter and form, substance and accident, cause and effect, simple and composite, subtle and dense, transparent and opaque, light and darkness, motion and rest, hot and cold, moist and dry, heavy and light, beneficial and harmful, good and evil, correct and erroneous, truth and falsehood, male and female—*"And of all things We created pairs."* (Quran 51:49)  

- **Triads**: Such as length, width, and depth; line, surface, and body; past, present, and future; necessary, possible, and impossible; mathematical, natural, and divine.  

- **Quartets**: Such as the four natures—heat, cold, moisture, and dryness; fire, air, water, and earth; blood, phlegm, yellow bile, and black bile; spring, autumn, summer, and winter. And so on, extending to higher units and tens.  

They say: **One** is the origin of all numbers, just as *God the Exalted* is the Creator of all existents. And just as the number one has no limit because it has no genus, so too *God the Exalted* has no limit because He has no genus. And just as one is present in all numbers, *God the Exalted* is present with all existents. And just as all numbers perish while one remains, all existents perish while *God the Exalted* endures. And just as numbers arise from the repetition of one, the existence of all things arises from the creation of God.  

**Two** is the first numerical pair and the foundation of calculation, just as the First Intellect is the origin of all other things.  

**Three** follows two in order, just as the Soul follows the Intellect.  
**Four** corresponds to prime matter (*hylē*).  
**Five** corresponds to Nature.  
**Six** corresponds to absolute body.  
**Seven** corresponds to the celestial spheres.  
**Eight** corresponds to the elements.  
**Nine** corresponds to generated beings.  

And just as nine is the last of the units, generated beings are the last of universal existents. Minerals correspond to the tens, plants to the hundreds, and animals to the thousands.  

Numbers are either even or odd, whole or fractional. Among their ranks:  
- That which belongs to the realm of spirits is best suited to oddness by nature.  
- That which belongs to the realm of bodies is best suited to evenness by nature.  
- That which belongs to the celestial spheres is best suited to wholeness by nature.  
- That which belongs to generation and corruption is best suited to fractions by nature.  

Know that **body** is one of the existents—a composite substance formed from two simple substances: matter (*hylē*) and form. **Matter** is the substance that receives form, while **form** is the substance that actualizes it. Matter is like iron, and form is like the shape of a knife. The first form that matter accepts is length, width, and depth. Through the mediation of body, it then accepts all other forms, such as the triangle, square, pentagon, and so on.  

Prime matter is an effect of the Soul, while the First Intellect is a substance brought into existence by the Necessary Being (*wājib al-wujūd*). It is a spiritual substance—eternal, complete, and perfect. The cause of its completeness and perfection is the emanation (*fayḍ*) of the Necessary Being, the cause of its permanence is the continuous outpouring of divine grace, the cause of its completeness is its receptivity to this emanation, and the cause of its perfection is its transcendence through the Necessary Being. Thus, the cause of the Soul’s existence is the Intellect.  

This is the opinion of the sage Pythagoras on this matter.  
*And God knows best.*
