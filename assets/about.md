# About Mujmal al-Hikma App

This application is a reader and research environment for the *Mujmal al-Hikma* (Compendium of Wisdom), an anonymous Persian transcreation of the _Epistles of the Brethren of Purity_, based on an Arabic original. The text of the _Mujmal_ presented here is based on Muhammad-Taqi Daneshpazhuh and Iraq Afshar's edition.

There are major differences between this work and the Arabic _Rasail Ikhwan al-Safa_ with many interventions on the part of the translator, such as omitting one entire epistle (_On Animals_), and combining and reframing others. The biggest liberty the translator has taken with Part IV, reducing it  to just one epistle. What remains of the epistles in this part form chapters that vaguely correspond to the original. This very curious choice reveals much about the author's intellectual commitments and taste.

 Otherwise, the general structure is the same as the _Rasāʾil Ikhwān al-Ṣafāʾ wa Khullān al-Wafāʾ_, again with some confusion, especially in part IV. The author-translator is upfront about these alterations and claims his intention is clarifying cryptic language and removing superfluous and useless uncientific content. 

 So far my reading suggests that the original Arabic _Mujmal al-hikma_ on which this translation is based represented a faithful abridgement of the _Rasāʾil Ikhwān al-Ṣafāʾ_ if not a version of the _Rasāʾil_ under another title. There were obvious reasons for dissociating the , or at least the non-ideological part of itt from the malighned anonymous authors. Ealiest manuscripts of the _Mujmal_ aslo diverge: some reference the _Rasāʾil_ and some do not. Overall, the Persian Ikhwanian tradition is very rich and worth studying on its own, but it is also hoped that the study of the _Mujmal_ will contributed to our understanding of the more famous Arabic tradition.

### The App

This app was created by Aslisho Qurboniev (2025) as part of research on the _Ikhwān al-Safāʾ_ corpus. The text is presented in its original Persian with AI powered English translations. Other features that will be added in due course. All these materials are shared solely for the purpose of research and colaboration.

The application features:

- Original Persian text with English translations   
- Manuscript images of the two texts
- Data visualizations of textual analysis
- Full-text search capabilities

### Next steps
The results will be presented at two conferences in Munster (Germany) and Tashkent (Uzbekistan) in 2025. The outcome will be an interactive digital tool and will also form a part of a peer-reviewed publication.
